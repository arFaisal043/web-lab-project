Our Story

From Bean to Cup

Welcome to Bean & Brew! We are more than just a coffee shop; we are a community of coffee enthusiasts dedicated to bringing you the finest brews from around the globe.

Founded in 2020 by a group of friends who shared a deep passion for ethically sourced, perfectly roasted beans, Bean & Brew has grown from a humble pop-up stand into your favorite online destination for all things coffee.

Ethical & Sustainable

We work directly with farmers in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala to ensure fair trade practices. Every cup you drink supports sustainable farming and helps preserve the rich, authentic flavors of the origins.

We expertly roast every batch in-house to highlight its unique profile. Whether you're starting your morning or taking a well-deserved afternoon break, we guarantee every sip will awaken your senses. Thank you for being part of our journey.
